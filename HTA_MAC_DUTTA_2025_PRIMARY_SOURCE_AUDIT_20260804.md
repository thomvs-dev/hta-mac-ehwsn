# Dutta et al. 2025 Primary-Source Competitor Audit

**Date:** 4 August 2026  
**Paper:** H. Dutta, A. K. Bhuyan, and S. Biswas, “Cooperative Reinforcement Learning for Energy Management in Multi-Hop Networks With Energy Harvesting,” *IEEE Transactions on Green Communications and Networking*, vol. 9, no. 4, pp. 1783–1793, 2025.  
**DOI:** [10.1109/TGCN.2025.3544073](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGCN.2025.3544073)  
**Primary manuscript:** [author-uploaded full text](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389184756_Cooperative_Reinforcement_Learning_for_Energy_Management_in_Multi-Hop_Networks_With_Energy_Harvesting)

## 1. Why this paper matters

This is a closer competitor to HTA-MAC than Dutta et al. 2024 because energy harvesting is part of both the simulator and the learned transmission state. It therefore invalidates broad novelty claims based on per-node harvest-aware learned transmit/sleep scheduling. It must be prominent in the introduction, related work, and mechanism-comparison table for an IEEE TGCN submission.

## 2. Mechanism recovered from the primary manuscript

### Network and harvesting model

- Decentralized multi-hop sensor/IoT mesh with multi-point-to-point traffic and fixed-size slotted MAC frames.
- Poisson packet arrivals and finite per-node queues.
- Solar harvesting generated by a two-state Markov model: high-radiation and low-radiation states.
- The Markov model generates the energy arrivals; the learned state is a quantized recent harvested-energy measure, not the transition-probability row or a Bayesian HMM posterior.
- The paper states that the approach could accept other harvesting sources, but its experiments use solar only. It does not validate a hybrid solar-plus-thermal model.

### Learning architecture

Two tabular Q-learning agents are deployed per node and share one reward:

1. **Transmission-scheduling agent**
   - State: discretized harvested-energy influx over the decision epoch.
   - Action: discretized packet-transmission probability in `[0,1]`.
2. **Sleep-scheduling agent**
   - State: transmission probability selected by the transmission agent.
   - Action: discretized probability that the transceiver remains on.

The agents jointly optimize missed receptions and queue stability. Nodes share a learning-confidence parameter with one-hop neighbors to suppress unreliable downstream updates during exploratory/oscillatory learning.

## 3. Direct comparison with HTA-MAC

| Dimension | Dutta et al. 2025 | HTA-MAC |
|---|---|---|
| Network | Decentralized multi-hop mesh | Clustered terrestrial WSN under frozen exogenous CH schedule |
| Harvesting | Solar, two-state Markov generator | Solar eight-state plus synthetic-auxiliary thermal four-state HMMs |
| RL harvest input | Quantized recent harvested-energy influx | Forecast moments plus solar/thermal state-conditioned transition vectors |
| Learner | Two tabular Q-learning agents per node | Shared Branching Dueling C51 network |
| Action | Transmission probability and transceiver-on probability | Integer per-node slot count `0..n_max` |
| Coupling | Shared reward and neighbor learning-confidence parameter | Shared trunk/branches plus deterministic cluster-budget projection |
| Resource constraint | Energy-aware stochastic transmit/sleep policies | Hard per-cluster TDMA slot budget |
| Slot allocation | Fixed-size slotted MAC; no learned multi-slot-count vector | Learned, budget-constrained per-node multi-slot counts |
| Evaluation | Multi-hop traffic, heterogeneous solar conditions | Frozen CH schedule, hybrid-harvest clustered MAC environment |

## 4. Claim consequences

The following claims are disallowed:

- first per-node harvest-aware learned transmit/sleep scheduler;
- first RL policy adapting transmission to Markov-modeled solar harvesting;
- first cooperative/learned energy management for multi-hop EH-WSNs;
- novelty based merely on combining Markov solar arrivals with RL.

A defensible, search-bounded distinction is:

> HTA-MAC combines per-node solar-and-thermal HMM state-transition features with a shared Branching Dueling categorical value network that selects a hard-budgeted vector of discrete intra-cluster slot counts under a frozen CH schedule; Dutta et al. instead use two decentralized tabular Q-learning agents per node to select transmission and transceiver-on probabilities from quantized solar-energy influx and transmission-policy state in a multi-hop mesh.

This distinction is architectural and problem-specific. It is not proof of empirical superiority.

## 5. Baseline decision

Dutta et al. 2025 should be a prominent qualitative competitor, not silently added as a post-hoc numerical baseline. A faithful implementation would require a multi-hop flow/queue simulator, two cooperative agents per node, confidence-parameter sharing, and probability-based transmit/sleep actions. Those semantics are materially different from the frozen clustered TDMA experiment. Any future implementation must be declared exploratory before results are viewed.

## 6. Remaining literature action

Before manuscript freeze, verify the final IEEE pagination/issue metadata and audit whether any 2025–2026 paper combines hybrid multi-source harvesting with learned discrete TDMA slot-count vectors. The novelty statement must retain “to the best of the documented search.”
