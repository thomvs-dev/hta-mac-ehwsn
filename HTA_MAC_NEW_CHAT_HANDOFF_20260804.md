## 2. Non-negotiable research scope

HTA-MAC is a MAC-only follow-on to HEART-CH.

- HEART-CH cluster-head selection is frozen and exogenous.
- Frozen per-round schedule replay is used for causal isolation of the MAC layer.
- Do not retrain CH selection.
- Do not implement routing, Pointer Networks, or joint CH/MAC/routing learning.
- Do not mix HERMES results or checkpoints into HTA-MAC. HERMES uses incompatible simulator assumptions.
- Report only results generated by the present repository and archived artifacts.
- A failed gate must be repaired or reported; it must never be bypassed.

The bounded contribution under investigation is a shared Branching Dueling C51 policy for hard-budgeted, per-node discrete intra-cluster slot counts using individual hybrid solar/thermal HMM state-transition features.

## 9. Death-signal decision

The training curriculum does observe death. Across Phase 2B lineages, death occurred in roughly 62-70% of episodes.

At a firing timestep, mean weighted death magnitude was approximately 2.07-2.13, versus typical packet-term magnitude approximately 1.36-1.50. The death/packet ratio was approximately 1.38-1.56.

Decision: retain w3 = 2.0. The signal is not numerically drowned when it fires. This does not prove correct long-horizon causal credit or deployment generalization.

The audit measures DynamicClusterTrainingEnv, not the network-wide Phase 3 evaluation regime. Treat curriculum death as local credit/stress evidence, never as network-wide lifetime evidence.

## 14. Open doubts and risks

1. Branch identity shortcut: the 100 node-indexed branches may memorize position/node history.
2. Return-scale drift: the frozen c fits repaired Phase 2B returns; improved policies could still approach support boundaries.
3. Curriculum/evaluation shift: local target-cluster training and network-wide control have materially different death frequencies.
4. Thermal realism: the thermal channel is synthetic auxiliary evidence.
5. Schedule replay: causal isolation is strong, but CH decisions cannot adapt to HTA-induced energy changes.
6. Queue/freshness trade-off: earlier HTA policy protected lifetime partly by sleeping, reducing service and increasing stale drops.
7. Final held-out set: earlier seeds 3100-3104 have already informed development.
8. Novelty: close TGCN competitors eliminate broad slot/sleep and Markov-harvest RL claims.
9. C4 dominance claim: strict dominance is not authorized until assumptions and Phase 5 empirical validation pass.
