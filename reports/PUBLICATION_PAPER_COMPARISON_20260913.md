# HTA-MAC publication comparison — 13 September 2026

## Assessment

The latest deterministic complete-frontier allocator has a repeatable, modest advantage over the author-constructed reserve-only comparator **inside the paid-control, paid-loss replay model**. It has not demonstrated superiority to an external paper or to frozen V1 under a matched simulator. This is useful mechanism evidence, but insufficient by itself for a strong journal claim of a broadly superior MAC protocol.

The prospective 20-seed replication (380000–380019; 40 trials) reports **+1.7077% conservative delivery and +1.8963% packets/J**. Conservative delivery compares the candidate's lower bound to the reference's upper bound for actually generated cohort packets. The paired absolute differences and Bonferroni-adjusted 97.5% t intervals are respectively **0.00156054 [0.00112982, 0.00199127]** and **8.21550 [7.60572, 8.82529] packets/J**. The delivery difference is about **0.1561 percentage points**, not 1.7077 percentage points. All ten predeclared screen checks passed. Source: [replication results](PAID_LOSS_REPLICATION_RESULTS_20260912.md), [machine-readable screen](../outputs/paid_loss_replication_20260912/screen.json).

The result covers 1,567–1,636 observed frames per seed, not the planned 3,000-frame horizon. Upstream HEART-CH termination remains informative; no missing future packets are invented. Each replay has 20 moving nodes out of 100. Both methods pay report, grant and ACK costs; member attempts and aggregate forwarding each have erasure probability 0.10. Reliable funded control/ACKs, abstract frame timing, and 24 data slots **per cluster** limit the claim. No new neural training or hardware validation is represented by this result. Historical Gates A/B and frozen V1 remain unchanged.

## Defensible contribution and remaining gaps

The candidate contribution is the **measured value of expanding the feasible service-total family while retaining the same reserve surrogate**, under paid observations, coupled member/CH energy feasibility, frozen mobile HEART-CH replays and explicit censor bounds. This is a candidate research position, not a verified claim of being the first such method. The allocator retains one canonical minimum-energy allocation per service total and the reserve incumbent; score dominance is restricted to that enumerated family. It is not a theorem of long-run delivery optimality or an exhaustive enumeration of all equal-energy allocations.

For a stronger journal submission, the next evidence should resolve these concrete gaps:

1. **External baselines:** obtain the full cooperative-RL formulation and implement a faithful compatible baseline, documenting every necessary adaptation. Include standard scheduling controls and ablations under the same paid model. Do not relabel the reserve-only method as a published algorithm.
2. **Observation horizon:** predeclare an independent full-horizon design or retain the explicitly limited observed-cohort estimand. Current confidence intervals address seed variation within this model, not bias from unseen future operation.
3. **Protocol realism:** specify report encoding, synchronization, fixed frame airtime, control/ACK losses, retransmissions and interference. The present energy charges are not a complete physical protocol implementation.
4. **Generality and cost:** predeclare different load, harvest, mobility and burst-loss regimes; report runtime/memory and information requirements as well as delivery, packets/J, fairness and staleness. Preserve all failed regimes.
5. **Independent validation:** without hardware, validate the energy/timing assumptions against an independent simulator or trace-based implementation and publish the reproduction assets. This can strengthen simulation evidence but cannot be described as hardware validation.

These are separate prospective studies with fresh seed reservations, not permission to retune on the opened replication or relax an existing gate. A modest positive effect with transparent limits is more defensible than a large cross-paper percentage assembled from incompatible settings.
